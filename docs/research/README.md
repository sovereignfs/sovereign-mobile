## What a research doc is

A research doc captures an open-ended question that doesn't yet have a
committed design: findings, options considered, and a recommendation —
lighter than a formal design doc, and kept after the decision lands. It's
the working record of _why_. Once a question is settled with binding
consequences, it graduates into an [ADR](../adrs/README.md); the research
doc stays as the record of how the decision was reached.

Not every research doc needs a definitive conclusion — "not now" or
"insufficient information yet" is a valid, useful outcome.
